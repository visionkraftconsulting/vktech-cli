Pipeline

Eight stages, zero clicks

Point it at a directory of clips. It does the rest and verifies the result.

01 · PROBE

Understand the footage

02 · PLAN

Build the cut

Windows long takes to their highlight; targets your runtime.

03 · CLEAN

Remove dark shots

Detects and cuts lens-down, covered, and black frames.

04 · CAPTION

AI titles

Vision AI reads the scene and writes tasteful on-screen cards.

05 · GRADE

Set the mood

06 · LEVEL

Balance audio

Loudness-normalizes every clip to a consistent level.

07 · ASSEMBLE
