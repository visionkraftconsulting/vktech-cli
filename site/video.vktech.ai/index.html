Pipeline

Eight stages, zero clicks

Point it at a directory of clips. It does the rest and verifies the result.

01 · PROBE

Understand the footage

Reads every clip, sorts by capture time into a timeline.

02 · PLAN

Build the cut

Windows long takes to their highlight; targets your runtime.

03 · CLEAN

Remove dark shots

Detects and cuts lens-down, covered, and black frames.

04 · CAPTION

AI titles

Vision AI reads the scene and writes tasteful on-screen cards.

05 · GRADE

Set the mood

Color presets — memorial, cinematic, wedding, vlog & more.

06 · LEVEL

Balance audio

Loudness-normalizes every clip to a consistent level.

07 · ASSEMBLE

Stitch losslessly

Joins everything into one master, captions composited.

08 · VERIFY

Prove it's clean

Full-decode integrity check — zero errors before you get it.
